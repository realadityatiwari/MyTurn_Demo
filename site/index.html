The problem

Waiting is manageable. Not knowing is the hard part.

  • Patientscan't tell whether they are registered, checked in, or actually in the line.
  • Front desksoften track arrivals, calls and late returns by hand, in several places.
  • Missed turnsleave everyone unsure what happens next, and what the clinic has promised.

How it works

One shared queue. Two views.

Patients follow their visit on their own phone. Clinic staff run the queue at the desk. Every action on either side updates the other.

  1. Register

    Choose a clinic and session, pick who the visit is for, and receive a token.

  2. Check in

    Check in on arrival. Registering alone never puts anyone in the waiting line.

  3. Follow your turn

    See your status and who is ahead, only when it can be shown reliably.

  4. Be called

    Staff call the next patient, start service and complete it, one patient at a time.

Missed your turn? Request to return. Staff decide under clinic policy, and the original place is never promised.

Benefits

Clear for the person waiting. Calm for the person running the desk.

For patients

  • A clear status at every step, in English or हिन्दी.
  • One account for up to 7 family members.
  • Honest information: no invented wait times.

For clinic staff

  • One queue that links registration, check-in and the desk.
  • Roles: operators run the queue, administrators handle overrides and resets.
  • Patients appear as first name and last initial only.
